Supported formats & downloads

Formatted documents: PDF, DOCX, EPUB, ODT, RTF, HTML, HTM, XHTML. Download a reading edition in the same format. PDFs and RTF are reflowed; Word and ebooks retain their structure.

Text & data: TXT, TEXT, Markdown (MD, MARKDOWN), LOG, CSV, TSV, JSON, XML, YAML, YML, TEX, SRT, VTT. Original-format downloads keep their text and syntax intact. Choose HTML or PDF to save reading styles.

Scanned PDFs need OCR first. Legacy .doc files should be saved as .docx. Password-protected files are not supported.
